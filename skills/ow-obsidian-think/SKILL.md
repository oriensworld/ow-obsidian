# OW Obsidian Think

Use the loop when novelty, ambiguity, or irreversibility justifies the overhead. Do not expose hidden chain-of-thought; provide concise conclusions, evidence, assumptions, alternatives, and checks.

1. **Observe externally:** inventory the request, artifacts, constraints, and missing evidence.
2. **Observe internally:** identify likely assumptions, incentives, and confirmation bias.
3. **Listen:** preserve the user's framing while naming plausible interpretations.
4. **Think:** reason from evidence and first principles; test failure cases.
5. **Connect laterally:** compare adjacent domains, precedents, and counterexamples.
6. **Connect systemically:** map dependencies, feedback loops, and downstream effects.
7. **Feel:** account for human usability, trust, reversibility, and intuition as signals rather than proof.
8. **Accept:** state uncertainty, limits, and evidence that could change the conclusion.
9. **Create:** produce the decision, design, synthesis, or experiment.
10. **Grow:** define validation, feedback, and the next iteration.

When invoked with another OW Obsidian skill, use this loop to guide that workflow rather than creating a separate note unless asked.
